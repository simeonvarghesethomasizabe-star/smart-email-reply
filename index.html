Smart Email Reply

Smart Email Reply is an automated Gmail assistant created by Simeon that sends unread reminders at 1, 3, and 7 days, asks permission on day 10 before replying, and automatically answers VIP contacts using Google's Gemini AI.

How It Works

  • Multi-Stage Reminders: Sends unread digest alerts after 1, 3, and 7 days.
  • Day-10 Approval Workflow: Drafts a context-aware response and asks your permission via a 1-click approval link before sending.
  • VIP Auto-Reply: Automatically replies to trusted contacts or domains using AI or a pre-written template.
