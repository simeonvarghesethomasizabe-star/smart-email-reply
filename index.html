Smart Email Reply

Smart Email Reply is a lightweight Gmail automation assistant created by Simeon that monitors unread primary emails older than 7 days and drafts or sends context-aware, polite follow-up replies using Google's Gemini AI.

How It Works

  • 7-Day Unread Check: Identifies important unread messages in your Primary inbox that have waited 7 days for a response.
  • Smart Spam & Bot Filtering: Automatically skips receipts, newsletters, alerts, and no-reply addresses.
  • Context-Aware Replies: Generates natural, tone-matched acknowledgments so senders know their message was received.
