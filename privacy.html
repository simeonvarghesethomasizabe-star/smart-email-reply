Privacy Policy for Smart Email Reply

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

1. Information We Access

Smart Email Reply accesses your Gmail account solely to search for unread messages older than 7 days in your Primary inbox, read message content to generate context-aware replies, apply tracking labels, and send responses on your behalf.

2. How Data Is Processed

Email snippets are transmitted securely to the Google Gemini API solely to generate reply text. Smart Email Reply does not store, log, sell, or share your emails or personal data on any external database or third-party server.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy

Smart Email Reply's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Revoking Access

You can revoke Smart Email Reply's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Account Permissions page.
